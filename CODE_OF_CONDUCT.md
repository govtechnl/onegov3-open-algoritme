# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Personal data, sensitive topics and registrations

This challenge works with a real government algorithm about family relationships. Some extra rules apply:

- **Fictional persons only.** Do not put real personal data, real BRP extracts or real citizen service numbers (BSN) in this repository, in issues, on Alkemio or in demos. This includes your own data and that of people you know. If a challenge owner or mentor tells your team about a real case, it stays out of your submission.
- **Care with the subject.** Gezag touches divorce, death, guardianship and court rulings. Choose examples, jokes and demo scenarios with care, also with fictional persons.
- **No verdicts on real parties.** Observations about a registration in the Algoritmeregister describe what a motivering needs. Do not present them as a judgement about the organisation that registered the algorithm or about its staff.
- **Nothing is sent.** Do not send generated messages to real algorithm owners, municipalities or residents. Drafts stay drafts.
- **No credentials.** Never commit API keys or tokens, including GreenPT credentials. Use environment variables and keep `.env` files out of git.
- **Honest labelling.** Do not present a simulated MijnOverheid message, chatbot answer or connection as real. Say what is simulated.
- **Respect for the people involved.** The challenge owners and mentors give their time to help teams. Their input during the hackathon is not a formal position of their organisation.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the organisers at [hack@govtechnl.nl](mailto:hack@govtechnl.nl) or to any organiser on site. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Organisers may remove contributions or exclude participants who violate this code.

For the full text of the Contributor Covenant, including the enforcement guidelines, see <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
